## Profile

I build applied AI systems for problems that affect daily life. My current work focuses on food waste reduction, online safety, computer vision, and field robotics. At Synventra, I lead projects from early research and prototyping through validation and deployment.

I was a scholar in the **28th Junior Science Talent Project (JSTP)** administered by the National Science and Technology Development Agency (NSTDA), Thailand.

## Research interests

- AI for food waste measurement and resource-efficient operations
- Thai-language AI and consumer protection
- Computer vision and multimodal machine learning
- Field robotics and public safety technology

## Selected work

- [**TaLaem.**](https://synventra.com/projects/talaem-ai-assistant-for-scam-online-threat-detection) I co-developed an AI assistant on LINE that assesses suspicious messages, screenshots, and voice calls for scams and online threats. The project received an Honorable Mention at AI for Thai Service Onboarding 2026, organized by the National Electronics and Computer Technology Center (NECTEC) under the National Science and Technology Development Agency (NSTDA).
- [**TERMTEM @PHUKET.**](https://synventra.com/news/synventra-at-bdi-hackathon-2026-our-kick-off-in-phuket) Development of an AI and data system for food waste management in tourism operations. Finalist in the 2026 PRO AT Phuket hackathon organized by the Big Data Institute (Public Organization), or BDI.
- **Omnidirectional Surface Mobility Apparatus for Remote Controlled Rescue Exploration on Rubble Terrain.** Thai Petty Patent No. 26405, Department of Intellectual Property, Thailand.
- **PuenMor.** Development of an artificial intelligence based risk and trend assessment system for neurological disorders. Funded by the Junior Science Talent Project (JSTP), National Science and Technology Development Agency (NSTDA).
- **SEESEED.** Development of a computer vision platform for corn seed quality grading. Super AI Engineer Season 5.
- **Nematodroid.** Development of an omnidirectional exploration robot with a vision system for disaster victim search. Supported by the National Science and Technology Development Agency (NSTDA) and the National Research Council of Thailand (NRCT).
- **TERMTEM @RAMA.** Campus water refill gamification for plastic reduction. Funded by the Ton Kla Ramathibodi Project, Faculty of Medicine Ramathibodi Hospital, Mahidol University.
