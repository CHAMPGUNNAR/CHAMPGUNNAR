## About

I am an AI researcher and technology developer studying **Computer Engineering and Digital Technology (CEDT)** at the Faculty of Engineering, Chulalongkorn University. I lead Synventra in developing practical, human-centered technology grounded in science and engineering.

My work sits at the intersection of artificial intelligence, computer vision, autonomous systems, and public-interest technology. I am also a scholar of the **28th Junior Science Talent Project (JSTP)** under Thailand’s National Science and Technology Development Agency (NSTDA).

## Selected work

- **Omnidirectional rescue mobility apparatus** — Thai Petty Patent No. 26405 for remote-controlled rubble exploration.
- **PuenMor** — AI-based risk and trend assessment for neurological disorders, funded by JSTP and NSTDA.
- **SEESEED** — Computer-vision platform for corn-seed quality grading, funded through Super AI Engineer Season 5, PMU-B, and NXPO.
- **Nematodroid** — Omnidirectional vision-enabled exploration robot for disaster victim search, supported by NSTDA and NRCT.
- **TERMTEM** — Campus water-refill gamification for plastic reduction, funded by the Ton Kla Ramathibodi Project.
